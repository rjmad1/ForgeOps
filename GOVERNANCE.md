# Project Governance

This document defines the governance model for the ForgeOps project.

## Roles and Responsibilities

### Contributors
Contributors are community members who submit pull requests, issues, feedback, or documentation. Contributors do not have direct write access to the repository, but their code is welcomed via reviews.

### Maintainers
Maintainers are active contributors who have demonstrated commitment, architectural understanding, and alignment with project goals. Maintainers have write access to the repository and are responsible for reviewing and merging pull requests.

### Steering Committee
The Steering Committee is composed of lead maintainers responsible for:
- Directing the overall architectural roadmap and vision.
- Resolving governance, code of conduct, and maintainer conflicts.
- Overseeing release planning and engineering standards.

---

## Decision-Making Process

Our goal is to make decisions through community consensus. We use the following mechanisms:

### Lazy Consensus
For minor changes, documentation updates, or standard bug fixes, maintainers employ **lazy consensus**. A pull request is approved if it receives at least one maintainer review and has no objections within 72 hours.

### Voting
For major architectural changes, dependencies, roadmaps, or governance additions, maintainers will initiate a vote on the issue/proposal:
- **Quorum**: Requires participation of at least 3 maintainers.
- **Approval**: Passed by a simple majority vote (+1 / -1) of the participants.

---

## Becoming a Maintainer

Contributors who consistently submit high-quality patches, assist other community members, and participate in reviews will be nominated by an existing maintainer to join the maintainers group. The nomination is approved if no active maintainers object within 7 days.
